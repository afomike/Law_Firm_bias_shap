What is Bias Auditing?

Bias auditing compares a model's predicted positive rates across demographic subgroups to detect systematic disparities. Demographic parity requires that each group receives positive predictions at roughly equal rates, independent of group membership. This audit examines gender as a protected attribute and flags statistically meaningful gaps that could indicate the model is encoding historic discrimination present in training data.
